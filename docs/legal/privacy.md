# Privacy policy

**Last updated:** 27 September 2026

## Who we are

Doubt (“we”) operates a Solana token discovery and analysis website and API.

## Data we process

### Automatically collected

- Standard web logs (IP address, user agent, timestamps) via hosting providers  
- Request paths (e.g. mints queried) for reliability and abuse prevention  

### You provide

- Contract addresses you paste or open  
- No account registration in the public alpha  

### We do not collect (alpha)

- Seed phrases or private keys  
- KYC documents  
- Portfolio holdings (unless you paste an address into a future feature explicitly designed for that)  

## Cookies

Essential cookies or local storage may be used for UI preferences (e.g. recent checks). We do not run third-party advertising trackers in the core product.

## Sharing

We use infrastructure processors (e.g. Vercel, GitHub) to host and ship the product. We do not sell personal information.

## Retention

Operational logs are retained only as long as needed for security, debugging, and legal compliance.

## Your choices

- Do not use the Service if you do not want addresses you query to hit our servers  
- Contact us via the official repository for deletion requests related to logs where feasible  

## International transfers

Hosting may process data in the United States or other regions.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change accordingly.

## Contact

GitHub Issues on the official repository.
